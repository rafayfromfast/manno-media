Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

Nutrition with Manno is an Instagram page that shares general nutrition information. This website and a small publishing tool ("Manno Page Automation") support that page. Contact: nutritionwithmanno@gmail.com.

What this website collects

This website has no accounts, forms, advertising or analytics, and sets no cookies. Like any web server, it keeps short-lived technical logs (IP address, time, page requested) to keep the service running and secure.

What the publishing tool does

The tool connects only to our own Instagram account, through Meta's official API, to publish our posts and to read how those posts perform in total (for example reach, likes, comments and saves). It does not collect, store or sell personal information about people who view, like or follow the page, and it does not send messages to anyone.

Services we use

Instagram (Meta) hosts the page and its posts. Google Drive and Google Sheets hold our own drafts and schedules. Anthropic's Claude helps draft our posts; no personal information about our audience is sent to it. Each service has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

What you share with us on Instagram, such as comments or messages, is handled by Instagram under its own policy, and you can delete it there at any time. To ask what we hold about you or to have it removed, see data deletion or email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
