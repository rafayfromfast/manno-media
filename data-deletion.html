Data deletion

Our publishing tool does not keep personal information about people who view or follow the page. It only publishes our own posts and reads their overall performance.

To remove something you shared

Comments and messages you sent us live on Instagram. You can delete them yourself in the Instagram app at any time.

To ask us to delete data

Email nutritionwithmanno@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and your Instagram username. We will confirm within 30 days what, if anything, we held and that it has been removed.

If you connected an account to our app

You can remove the app's access in Instagram under Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites. Removing access also ends any data the app could read from that account.
